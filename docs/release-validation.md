## Record before testing

Record actual tester/date, machine identifier/classification, Windows version/build, x64 architecture, RAM, supported physical scaling settings and monitor count. Record each approved construction PDF's name, SHA-256, bytes, page count, vector/scan classification and an independent estimator's expected lengths/areas/wall areas/counts/prices. Link actual screenshots/logs/registration inventories and readback exports. Use disposable project copies and approved fixtures for interruptions. Do not terminate a process containing real work or change global security settings.

Use independent known values, for example a certified 10 m span, 10 m × 10 m = 100 m² square and 10 m × 3 m = 30 m² wall. For 2 m² at material 100 and labor 50 per m² with 30% selling markup, expected material 200, labor 100, cost 300, price 390 and margin 90. A PDF must actually encode the corresponding landmarks and approved scale; writing these numbers into evidence does not establish a fixture. Compare displays, reopen and internal CSV/XLSX readback against the same oracle. Record tolerance in each numeric check (at most 0.1% for validation); round display money consistently and retain exact raw values where available.
